# 001 · Every durood counts

Launch promo. 56 s, 1080 × 1920, night-sky style: the Green Dome under the stars, gold calligraphy, a light rising for every durood, the app in dark mode. Sound effects only (wind, whooshes, a bead click per tap, soft impacts); no music. Cover: `poster.jpg`.

## Text to post

> Allah and His angels send durood upon the Prophet ﷺ. One durood from you, and Allah sends ten blessings upon you.
> Now imagine a whole nation, together: 10 crore durood in 40 days, every one dedicated to the Seerat Circles across Pakistan.
> Count every durood with Seerah Quest. Ask a member for an invite code.
>
> اللہ اور اس کے فرشتے نبی ﷺ پر درود بھیجتے ہیں۔ آپ کے ایک درود پر اللہ دس رحمتیں نازل فرماتا ہے۔ آئیے پوری قوم مل کر 40 دن میں 10 کروڑ درود پڑھے۔ سیرت کوئسٹ پر ہر درود گنیں۔ دعوتی کوڈ کسی رکن سے لیں۔

## Before posting

- **Check the Quran and hadith text with a scholar.** The ayah is shown without the pause mark and in plain (imla'i) spelling; the Urdu renderings are short translations.
- The 10 crore / 40 days figures are the app's challenge defaults; change them in `scene.html` if the organisers set others.
- The members, caravan and numbers on the phone are a seeded demo, not real people.
- The Green Dome and minarets are a drawn silhouette, not a photograph.
- Sound: effects only. To post silent, render with `--silent`; a voice reciting the ayah over the first 9 seconds would suit it well.
