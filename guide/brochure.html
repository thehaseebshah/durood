A day with Seerah Quest

MorningRecite and add your count. Your goal bar starts to fill.
DaytimeDo the Sunnah of the day; tick it, share a photo.

Durood counter

Three ways to add, one tap to undo

After you recite, add the number. Days follow Pakistan time, and your goal bar fills as you go.

12345
  1. Today's numberBig and bold, rolling up as you add.
  2. Your daily goalThe bar fills; reach it and it bursts with a chime.
  3. Type a total, press AddThe main way: recite, then type how many.
  4. +100 · +500 · +1,000Quick amounts that add up in the box.
  5. Count by tappingA big button, one tap per durood. A buzz at every 33, a chime at 100.
Undo

Added by mistake? Tap UNDO in the message, or edit today and yesterday in History.

Add from anywhere

The green + at the top opens the counter on any screen.

No signal? No problem

Your durood waits safely on the phone and is sent once, by itself, when you are back online.

Tap counter, Undo, and the + sheet. Counts are on the honour system: recite for the sake of Allah and His Messenger ﷺ, not for ranking.

Sunnah & Seerah

Live one Sunnah. Learn one lecture.

Under the counter on Today, every day: a Sunnah to do, the lecture of the week and a moment to reflect.

Sunnah of the day

Read it with its source, do it, tick it +20. Add a photo of the Sunnah itself +40: no faces, location removed.

Lecture of the week

Watch in the app, answer the quiz, claim +150. Then leave one comment +20.

The Feed

News, reflections, photos and milestones. Like, reply, say MashaAllah, or report.

Daily reflection

Seerat Circles

Find a circle to study the Seerah in person. Join +300, teach +1000 with the Circle Teacher badge.

Together

Your caravan and your Qabila

Two kinds of group. A caravan is the group you choose. Your Qabila is the family tree of everyone you brought in.

Caravan · کارواں

A group you name

  • Create one, or search and ask to join
  • Invite members by username; the owner decides
  • See who has recited today, share milestones
  • A caravan streak when half recite each day
  • One caravan at a time

Qabila · قبیلہ

Everyone you brought in

  • Your username is your invite code
  • Share on WhatsApp in one tap
  • Everyone below you, at every level, counts
  • +500 when someone you invited reaches 100
  • Its own totals, milestones and badges

Al-Burhan edition

Tasks, routines, murabbi and masul

For Al-Burhan members, the same app also carries the work of guidance and volunteering.

Your own, or given by your masul. Tick the circle, swipe to finish or move a deadline. Points for keeping commitments.
