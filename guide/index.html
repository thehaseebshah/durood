Seerah Quest Guide

Learn Seerah Quest, one small step at a time

Seerah Quest helps us send durood on the Prophet ﷺ every day, and grow closer to him through Sunnah and Seerah. These short tutorials show you every screen with a picture, so you always know where to tap.

Start with tutorial 1 → I already have an account Download the PDF
New to apps like this?

Read tutorials 1 to 4 in order. They take about 10 minutes together. After that, open any tutorial when you need it.

Good to know

Seerah Quest is free and never asks you for money. You need an invite code to join: usually the username of the person who told you about it.

Getting started

  1. 1Put the app on your phoneOpen Seerah Quest in your browser and add it to your home screen, so it opens like any other app.2 min
  2. 2Join with an invite codeCreate your account with the code of the person who invited you, or ask an organiser for one.3 min
  3. 3Add your duroodAfter you recite, type how many and press Add. Or tap a quick amount, or count with your finger.3 min
  4. 4Find your way aroundThe four buttons at the bottom, the Browse menu, dark mode and Urdu.3 min

Every day

  1. 5Correct a wrong numberChange today's or yesterday's entries from History. Every change stays on the record.2 min
  2. 6Streaks, quests, points and badgesHow your streak, daily quests, points, levels and badges work, in plain words.4 min
  3. 7Sunnah, lectures and reflectionsDo the Sunnah of the day, watch the weekly Seerah lecture, write a reflection and read the Feed.4 min

Together

  1. 8Create or join a caravanA caravan is a small group you name. Members see each other's progress and rank together.4 min
  2. 9Invite people to your QabilaShare your invite code on WhatsApp. Everyone who joins through you, and everyone they bring, is your Qabila.3 min
  3. 10Rankings, leagues and the 40-day challengeSee how you, your caravan and your city are doing this week, and how close the community is to 10 crore.3 min

Your account

  1. 11Your profile, reminders and passwordAdd a photo, set your daily goal, city and reminder time, change your password, or delete your account.3 min
  2. 12Tasks, murabbi and masul (Al-Burhan)For Al-Burhan members:your tasks and routines, and connecting with your murabbi and masul.4 min
